Prompt-template notes
=====================
v4 legacy Franka lite:
- Path: /storage/wenzheng/showrobot/AgentRobot/prompts/v4/franka_mvtoken_lite.txt
- Used for: new ZWZ samples in easy_spatial and plain 0723+0724 exchange_token.
- Camera wording: Agentview overhead view; Wristview close-up view.
- Action wording: only lists the valid action tokens and high-level rules. It does
  not define MV_FWD/MV_BACK/MV_LEFT/MV_RIGHT coordinate semantics explicitly.

v2 Franka:
- Path: data/agentrobot/zwz_process_code/prompt_franka_zwz_v2.txt
- Camera wording: AgentView overhead/global view; WristView close-up view.
- AgentView MV_FWD: closer to the AgentView camera, away from robot body.
- AgentView MV_BACK: farther from the AgentView camera, toward robot body.
- WristView MV_FWD: toward bottom of WristView image, away from robot body.
- WristView MV_BACK: toward top of WristView image, toward robot body.

v2 Piper:
- Path: data/agentrobot/zwz_process_code/prompt_piper_zwz_v2.txt
- Camera wording: AgentView first-person/front view; WristView close-up view.
- AgentView MV_FWD: away from robot body, toward far side of AgentView image.
- AgentView MV_BACK: toward robot body, toward near side of AgentView image.
- WristView MV_FWD: toward top of WristView image, away from robot body.
- WristView MV_BACK: toward bottom of WristView image, toward robot body.

v3 Franka:
- Path: data/agentrobot/zwz_process_code/prompt_franka_zwz_v3.txt
- Same content/hash as v2 Franka.

v3 Piper:
- Path: data/agentrobot/zwz_process_code/prompt_piper_zwz_v3.txt
- Purpose: Piper MV_FWD/MV_BACK wording follows the exchanged visual frame in
  02_exchange_token.
- AgentView MV_FWD: toward robot body, toward near side of AgentView image.
- AgentView MV_BACK: away from robot body, toward far side of AgentView image.
- WristView MV_FWD: toward bottom of WristView image, toward robot body.
- WristView MV_BACK: toward top of WristView image, away from robot body.
